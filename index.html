About

My engineering path started on the shop floor at Bosch, where I learned how real manufacturing systems behave under pressure: tolerances, uptime, and process reliability are not abstract ideas, they are daily constraints. Later, at Tata Consultancy Services, I worked on mission-critical Azure and SQL infrastructure for an oil & gas client, where one issue could disrupt production and create immediate operational impact. That combination of hands-on manufacturing and high-availability cloud systems taught me how to design for connected, data-driven operations rather than isolated components. It is the reason I now focus on robotics, edge intelligence, and industrial systems that need to work reliably in the real world.
